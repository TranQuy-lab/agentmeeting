GitHub Bug Bounty

Software security researchers are increasingly engaging with internet companies to hunt down vulnerabilities. Our bounty program gives a tip of the hat to these researchers and provides rewards of $10,000 or more in our public program, and $30,000 or more in our private program for critical vulnerabilities.

If you have found a vulnerability, submit it here.

You can find useful information in our rules, scope, targets and FAQ sections.

Happy hacking!
